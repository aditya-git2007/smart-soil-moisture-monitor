# Smart Irrigation System — Wiring & Relay Isolation

## ⚡ Electrical Safety & Power Guidelines
* **Relay Isolation:** The water pump draws high inductive current. Always power the DC water pump from an external independent 5V power supply rather than directly from the microcontroller's 5V pin to prevent voltage dips and resets.
* **Sensor Placement:** Ensure the capacitive sensor body is inserted vertically into the soil up to the maximum fill line, keeping the upper circuit dry.
